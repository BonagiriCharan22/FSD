About Skill Academy

Institute History

Skill Academy was established in 2018 with the aim of providing quality technical education and professional training. Over the years, we have trained hundreds of students in programming, web development, data science, and emerging technologies.

Vision

To become a leading training institute that empowers students with industry-relevant skills and prepares them for successful careers.

Mission

To provide practical learning experiences, expert mentorship, and career-focused training programs that bridge the gap between academics and industry requirements.
